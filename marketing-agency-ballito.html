Questions

Ballito FAQs

Do you work with businesses outside Ballito on the North Coast?

Yes. We work with businesses across the Dolphin Coast and north of Durban, including Salt Rock, Shaka's Rock and Zimbali, as well as greater Durban.

How do I get more visitors to find my Ballito business on Google?

Start with a complete, verified Google Business Profile: the right category, accurate hours (including holiday hours), good photos and genuine reviews. Visitors rely heavily on Google Maps when they're somewhere new.

When should a Ballito business start marketing for the holiday season?

Ideally several weeks before. SEO and reviews take time to build, and social media works best when people already recognise you by the time they arrive.
