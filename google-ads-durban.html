Google Ads FAQs

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your industry and area. We'll recommend a starting budget in your free audit. Your ad spend is paid directly to Google, separately from our management fee.

How quickly will I get leads?

Ads can start showing as soon as the campaign is approved and live. The first few weeks are about learning what converts, then we optimise.

I tried Google Ads before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?

The most common causes are broad targeting, missing negative keywords and no conversion tracking. We fix those first, so every rand is accounted for.

Do you lock me into a contract?

No long-term contracts. You stay because it's working.
