When a customer can't get a quick answer, many simply move on to the next business.

A well-built chatbot answers common questions straight away (services, hours, areas you cover, how to book) and then collects the customer's details so you can follow up. It doesn't replace you. It makes sure nobody slips through the cracks while you're on a job, in a meeting or asleep.

We set it up around your real business information, test it carefully, and make it easy for customers to reach a real person. Chatbots work best alongside a strong website and SEO that bring visitors in.

A good fit if you…

  • Get the same customer questions over and over
  • Miss enquiries after hours or while you're busy
  • Get more WhatsApp messages than you can answer quickly
  • Want to capture more leads from the visitors you already get

Questions

Chatbots & AI FAQs

Will a chatbot give customers wrong answers?

We set it up only around information you approve, test it before launch, and configure it to hand over to you when it isn't sure instead of guessing.

Can it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. We can set up automated replies and FAQs for WhatsApp Business, with an easy way for customers to reach you directly.

What about customers' personal information?

We set chatbots up to collect only what you need to follow up, and help you handle that information in line with POPIA.

How much does it cost?

It depends on where it runs (website, WhatsApp or both) and how much it needs to do. We'll give you a clear quote after a free consultation.
