SEO FAQs

How much does SEO cost?

Our 90-Day Google Growth Plan is a one-time R15 000 and covers the audit, strategy, local SEO, website optimisation, content strategy and monthly reporting. We'll confirm the right scope after your free audit.

How long until I see results?

SEO builds momentum over time. Within 90 days we aim to have the foundations in place and rankings moving; competitive industries can take longer. Your free audit will give you an honest picture of your market.

Can you guarantee the number one spot?

No honest agency can guarantee a specific Google position, because Google controls the rankings. What we do commit to is doing the work that moves rankings and showing you clearly what's changing.

Do I need a new website?

Not usually. Most sites can be improved. If something is seriously holding you back, we'll tell you in the audit.
