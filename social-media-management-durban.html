Social media FAQs

Which platforms do you manage?

Usually Facebook and Instagram, and others like LinkedIn or TikTok where your customers are. We'll recommend the right mix in your audit.

How much does social media management cost?

It depends on how many platforms and posts you need. We'll give you a clear monthly quote after your free audit — no long-term contract.

Do I need to supply photos?

Photos of your real work, team and premises perform best, so we'll guide you on what to capture. We can also create graphics and use quality stock where it fits.

Will I approve posts before they go out?

Yes. You'll see and approve the content plan before anything is published.
