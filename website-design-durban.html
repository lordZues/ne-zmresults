Website design FAQs

How long does a new website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content is ready. We'll give you a clear timeline after your free consultation.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need. We'll give you a clear, fixed quote before any work starts.

Can you improve my existing website instead of building a new one?

Yes. Often an upgrade, with better speed, mobile layout, clearer content and stronger calls to action, gets you most of the benefit at a lower cost.

Will my new website show up on Google?

We build every site to be search-friendly and connect it to Google Search Console. To rank for competitive searches, most businesses pair their website with our SEO service.
